SLIDE — WHY USE KAFKA?
Why use Kafka?

Built for scale. Built for speed.

High Throughput
Deliver messages at network-limited throughput using a cluster of machines, with latencies as low as 2 ms.
Scalable
Scale to hundreds of brokers, trillions of messages per day, and petabytes of data — with no downtime during rebalancing.
Permanent Storage
Store event streams in a distributed, durable, fault-tolerant cluster. Replay any event at any point in time.
High Availability
Stretch clusters across availability zones or regions. Aiven handles the ops so you get the uptime, without the toil.
